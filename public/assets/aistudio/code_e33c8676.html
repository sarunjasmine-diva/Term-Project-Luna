For Takashimaya Cash Smart Card application, please visit our Customer Service Centre, at Level 3 with:

  • A recent colour passport-sized photograph
  • Your NRIC* (for Singaporeans / PRs), Employment Pass / Work Permit Pass / Dependant Pass (for foreigners)
  • Your NRIC or passes must show your permanent address in Singapore.

For DBS Takashimaya Cards, please update your details through DBS Digibank or the DBS website. For Takashimaya Cash Smart Cardholders, please visit our Customer Service Centre at Level 3 to submit an address change request form.
Takashimaya Cash Smart Card points are awarded exclusively when purchases are settled via cash, NETS, or Takashimaya Gift Vouchers. Transactions made with other credit cards do not qualify for Cash Smart bonus points accumulation.
You can ensure your PDPA marketing consent preferences are active by visiting our Customer Service Centre at Level 3, or by updating your communication settings via Takashimaya Online account portal.

BONUS POINTS

Double Bonus Points Promotion is not applicable for payment by and/or purchase of Takashimaya gift vouchers, purchases from BVLGARI, Cold Storage, Suju Dining Japanese Restaurant, Best Denki (Level 5), Presto Drycleaner, Starbucks, Takashimaya Shopping Centre specialty stores, restaurants, services, and other selected tenants as stipulated by Takashimaya Department Store from time to time.
Most standard retail merchandise purchases qualify. Certain services, concessions, and food outlets may carry exemptions. Please check in-store at cashier points for details.
Bonus points have validity periods tied to card statement cycles and program calendar years. Unredeemed bonus points will expire according to standard program terms. Cardholders are encouraged to redeem in blocks of 100 points.
Bonus Points cannot be directly offset at regular cashier counters. Instead, present your physical card and NRIC/ID at the Customer Service Centre on Level 3 to convert every 100 points into S$30 Takashimaya Gift Vouchers.

GIFT VOUCHERS

Takashimaya Gift Vouchers redeemed via bonus points do not carry an expiration date and remain valid for future visits across the department store.
Takashimaya Gift Vouchers can be utilized at participating Specialty Stores in Takashimaya Shopping Centre from Basement 2 to Level 4, as well as throughout the main Takashimaya Department Store.
